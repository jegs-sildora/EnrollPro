# DepEd JHS Stakeholder Interview Guide (Negros Occidental)

This interview guide is designed for the Registrar, Grade Coordinators, and Principal of our beneficiary public Junior High School. It covers every core process of the DepEd JHS Enrollment and Sectioning Management System, with questions translated into standard conversational Hiligaynon (Bacolod City).

## 1. Introduction & Ice Breaker

**English Intent:** Create a polite opening statement thanking them for their time. Explain that we want to understand their manual workflows from start to finish to ensure our digital system perfectly aligns with their needs.

**Hiligaynon Translation:**
> "Maayong aga/hapon, Sir/Ma'am. Madamo gid nga salamat sa inyo oras kag sa pagpaunlak sa amon. Ang katuyuan sang amon interview subong amo nga maintiendihan gid namon sing maayo ang inyo manual nga proseso halin sa umpisa asta sa katapusan. Gusto namon masiguro nga ang ginahimo namon nga digital system magatugma gid kag makabulig gid sa inyo adlaw-adlaw nga kinahanglanon."

---

## 2. System Setup & Personnel Management (SF7)

**English Intent:** How do you currently track your teachers' Bachelor's majors/minors and Postgraduate degrees?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Sir/Ma'am, paano ninyo gina-track ukon gina-record subong ang mga majors, minors, sa Bachelor's kag Postgraduate degrees sang inyo mga teachers?"

**English Intent:** How do you assign and track ancillary roles (e.g., MRF Coordinator, Grade Level Head) for the School Form 7 (SF7)?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Paano ninyo gina-assign kag gina-track ang mga ancillary roles, pareho sang MRF Coordinator ukon Grade Level Head, nga ibutang sa inyo School Form 7 (SF7)?"

**English Intent:** How do you decide the maximum capacity or slots for your specific curricular programs each year?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Paano kamo naga-decide kon pila lang ang maximum capacity ukon slots para sa inyo mga specific nga curricular programs (pareho sang STE ukon BEC) kada school year?"

---

## 3. Special Curricular Programs (SCP) Admission

**English Intent:** How do you manage applications for STE, SPA, and SPS before the official enrollment starts?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Paano ninyo gina-manage ang mga applications para sa mga SCP pareho sang STE, SPA, kag SPS antes gid mag-umpisa ang official nga enrollment?"

**English Intent:** What is your process for waitlisted students, and how do you handle it when a qualified student withdraws or forfeits their slot?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Ano ang inyo proseso para sa mga waitlisted nga estudyante? Kag ano ang inyo ginahimo kon may qualified nga estudyante nga mag-atras ukon mag-forfeit sang ila slot sa program?"

---

## 4. General JHS Enrollment & Deficiencies

**English Intent:** How do you process continuing/promoted (Grades 8-10) students versus Walk-ins, Transferees, and Balik-Aral learners?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Sir/Ma'am, paano ninyo gina-process ang enrollment sang mga continuing ukon promoted nga estudyante (Grades 8-10) kumpara sa mga walk-in, transferees, kag Balik-Aral learners?"

**English Intent:** How do you track and enroll students who have Back Subject Deficiencies (failed subjects from previous years)?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Paano ninyo gina-track kag gina-enroll ang mga estudyante nga may back subject deficiencies ukon may mga bagsak nga subject halin sa nagligad nga school year?"

**English Intent:** How do you handle the collection and verification of the Learner Enrollment and Survey Form (LESF) and birth certificate details (like the mother's maiden name)?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Paano ninyo gina-handle ang pag-kolekta kag pag-verify sang Learner Enrollment and Survey Form (LESF) kag sang mga detalye sa birth certificate sang bata (pareho sang maiden name sang nanay)?"

---

## 5. Class Advisership & Section Management

**English Intent:** How are physical class sections created, and how do you decide which teacher becomes the Class Adviser for a specific section?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Paano gina-create ang inyo physical nga class sections, kag paano kamo naga-decide kon sin-o nga teacher ang mangin Class Adviser sang isa ka specific nga section?"

**English Intent:** What happens if a section reaches its maximum capacity but there are still enrolled learners?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Ano ang nagakatabo kon ang isa ka section napuno na ukon nag-abot na sa maximum capacity pero may mga enrolled learners pa nga wala sang section?"

---

## 6. Auto-Sectioning & Masterlist Generation (SF1)

**English Intent:** Who is responsible for sorting the students into sections?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Sin-o sa inyo ang responsable ukon in-charge sa pag-sort kag pag-assign sang mga estudyante sa ila mga sections?"

**English Intent:** Do you use a snake-draft (heterogeneous) method for the Regular BEC? How do you ensure the Male-to-Female ratio and academic performance are balanced?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Naga-gamit bala kamo sang snake-draft ukon heterogeneous nga pamaagi para sa Regular BEC? Paano ninyo ginasiguro nga balanse ang Male-to-Female ratio kag ang ila academic performance sa sulod sang kada section?"

**English Intent:** Do you create "Temporary/Draft" sections first before finalizing the official School Form 1 (SF1)? How do you handle late enrollees who arrive after the lists are finalized?
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Naga-create man kamo anay sang 'Temporary' ukon 'Draft' nga sections bag-o gid ninyo i-finalize ang official nga School Form 1 (SF1)? Paano ninyo gina-handle ang mga late enrollees nga naga-abot pagkatapos nga na-finalize na ang mga masterlist?"

---

## 7. Closing

**English Intent:** A polite closing statement asking if they have any major pain points, bottlenecks, or tedious paperwork they desperately want our system to solve, followed by a professional thank you.
**Hiligaynon:** 
> "Antes kita magtapos, Sir/Ma'am, may ara bala kamo sang mga dalagku nga problema, bottlenecks, ukon mga tedious nga papeles nga kapoy gid obrahon manual nga gusto ninyo masolbar gid sang amon system? Madamo gid nga salamat liwat sa inyo tion kag sa pagbulig sa amon."
